The Genestack Operations Guide helps you answer _"What happens next?"_ after you deploy Genestack. 

After a successful deployment you will need to prepare for day-two operations. Day-two operations include triage, ongoing maintenance of deployed assets, rolling out upgrades, and troubleshooting.

The Genestack Operations Guide will help you:

  - Keep up to date with your service-level agreement (SLA) or service-level objective (SLO) requirements.
  - Troubleshoot customer support requests.
  - Stay current with the latest platform features and security updates.
  - Plan for future growth.

## Prerequisites

The Genestack Operations guide assumes that you deployed Genestack in accordance with the [Deployment Guide](../deployment-guide/) and are ready to focus on day-two workflows.
